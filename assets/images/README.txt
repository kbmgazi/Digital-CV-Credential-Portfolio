PROFILE PHOTO
==============

Add your professional profile photo to this folder, e.g.:

  profile-placeholder.jpg

Then open index.html, find the "about-photo" section, and:

1. Remove or comment out the <div class="photo-placeholder">...</div> block
2. Add: <img src="assets/images/profile-placeholder.jpg" alt="Your Name">

Recommended: a square or 4:5 portrait-oriented photo, at least 800px
on the shorter side, professionally lit and business-appropriate.
